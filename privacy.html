Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

WHIS (“WHIS”, “the app”, “I”) is a personal concert-history app operated by Alex Fiehrer, an individual, in the United States. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who else receives it, and the choices you have. In short: your concert history and photos are private to your account, nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or tracking in the app.

1. Information WHIS collects

  • Account information. Your email address and password when you create an account. The password is handled by the authentication service and is stored only in hashed form; WHIS cannot read it.
  • Content you add. The concerts you log (event name, venue, city and state, date, the artists you saw, ratings, favorites and notes), photos you choose to add (and the photo of an artist you pick), and your chosen theme.
  • Technical information. Like any online service, the servers that run WHIS receive your IP address and basic request details when the app connects. WHIS does not include advertising or tracking software. If the app crashes, a crash report (the error and its technical stack trace, the app version, and your device model and operating system version) is sent to Sentry, the error-reporting service. It does not include your name, email, account, IP address, photos or concert data, and it is used only to find and fix bugs.
  • Stored on your device. Your sign-in session, your font preference, a cache of photos and of city locations, and whether you have seen the intro. These stay on your phone.

The app asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo, and only reads the photos you pick. If you use “Read a poster or flyer” on the lineup screen, the picture you choose is read on your phone by the phone’s own text-recognition (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android) and is not uploaded or saved; only the text it finds is placed in the box for you to check. WHIS does not collect your location, contacts or any other personal data.

2. How the information is used

Only to provide the app: to show you your history, photos, statistics and map; to let you sign in and reset a forgotten password; to look up events, venues, cities and artists while you type; and to keep the service secure and working. WHIS does not use your information for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it for others' marketing.

4. Photos and shared catalog entries

Your photos belong to you and are visible only to your account. Photos may contain information your camera embeds (such as the time or location it was taken); WHIS does not read or publish it. The names of artists and venues you type are stored in a catalog shared across WHIS so the same name can be reused; those catalog entries are not linked to your account and are not removed when you delete it.

5. How long information is kept

  • Your information is kept for as long as you have an account.
  • A concert you delete is held for 7 days so you can restore it, and is then permanently removed. Its photo files are removed shortly afterwards.
  • When you delete your account (Settings > Account > Delete account), your account, concerts, photos and profile are permanently deleted. Backups held by the hosting provider may retain a copy for a limited time before they expire.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and download. Settings > Export history gives you a CSV or JSON copy of your concerts.
  • Correct. Edit any concert, or change your password in Settings.
  • Delete. Delete individual concerts or photos, or your whole account, in the app. If you cannot open the app, email whohaveiseen@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my WHIS account” and it will be deleted within 30 days. See also the support page.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area) you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to or restrict its use. Email the address below to exercise them; WHIS will not discriminate against you for doing so.

7. Children

WHIS is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, email the address below and the account will be deleted.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, photos are stored privately, and access to your data is restricted to your signed-in account. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. International use

WHIS is operated from the United States and your information is processed and stored there. By using the app you understand your information will be transferred to and stored in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way (for example, if analytics or purchases are added), the effective date above will be updated and the app will tell you.

Questions or requests: whohaveiseen@gmail.com.
