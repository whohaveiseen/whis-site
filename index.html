Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Logging and looking back on your shows is free, with no ads. A paid extra may come later for things like more photo storage; the diary itself will stay free.

Who can see my history?

Only you. Everything is private to your account. WHIS has no feed, no followers and no public profile.

Is a lineup photo uploaded?

No. The picture is read on your phone by the phone’s own text recognition, and only the text it finds is used.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export everything to a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full backup (JSON) from Settings, and import it back. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app too.

Which phones?

iPhone and Android.

Who makes it?

WHIS is made by one person who got tired of forgetting who they’d seen. Questions or ideas: whohaveiseen@gmail.com.
