Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas: whohaveiseen@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and what you were doing.

How to delete your account and data

In the app: open Settings → scroll to Account → tap Delete account → type DELETE and confirm. Your account, every concert, and all your photos are permanently deleted straight away.

Can't open the app? Email whohaveiseen@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my WHIS account”. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days and you will get a confirmation.

To delete just one concert or photo, open it in the app and choose Delete or the × on the photo. A deleted concert can be restored for 7 days and is then removed permanently. Artist and venue names you typed are kept in an anonymous shared catalog that is not linked to you. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Common questions

How do I get a copy of my data? Settings → Export history (CSV for a spreadsheet, JSON for a full backup).

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password?” and follow the email. Signed in already? Settings → Account → Change password.

Who can see my photos and history? Only you. They are private to your account.
